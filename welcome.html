Dinghy Sailing Examiner

The objective of this exam is to determine your competency in sailing a dinghy.

You must be able to sail a three-legged race in 6 minutes or less to pass the exam.

Instructions:

There are three buoys laid on the race course.

Your goal is to:

  1. Round the Windward Mark by sailing upwind.
  2. Round the Gybe Mark by sailing downdind.
  3. Sail to the Leeward Mark to finish the exam.

The Danger of the Downwind "Death Roll"

In this simulator, sailing directly downwind in brisk conditions presents an immediate risk of a Death Roll—a rapid, violent capsize to windward. It occurs when aerodynamic instability causes the boat to wobble uncontrollably side-to-side before completely rolling over.

  • 💨 Wind Strength: Constant 15 Knots (Heavy enough to induce strong sail rolling forces)
  • ⛵ Point of Sail: "Run" / "Running" (Sailing dead downwind maximizes hull instability; the only safe sailor position at 15 knots is Aft)
  • 🧍 Sailor Position: "Mid Center", "Forward", or "Leeward" (Failing to move your weight back)

🛡️ How to Survive: If the boat begins oscillating, you must immediately shift your GUI control selector to the "Aft" position to sink the stern, lift the bow, and regain control!
