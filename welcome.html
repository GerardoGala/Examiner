Dinghy Sailing Examiner

The objective of this exam is to determine the student's competency in sailing a dinghy.

You must be able to sail a three-legged race in 6 minutes or less to pass the exam.

Instructions:

There are three buoys laid on the race course.

Your goal is to:

1. Round the Windward Mark by sailing upwind and then round the Gyb

2. Round the Gybe Mark by sailing downdind

1. Sail to the Leeward Mark to finish the exam
